## What we can and cannot vouch for

**What we checked.** Every clip was downloaded by us from the source's own host and verified to be a real
video file (container magic bytes, not an HTML error page). Every prompt is quoted verbatim from the
source; none was translated, rewritten or summarised by us.

**What we are relying on.** The licence above is the *publisher's* grant. Most of these clips were
published by an aggregator that collected them from community creators, so the grant comes from the
collector rather than from the person who made the clip. We record the author the publisher credited and
link to the original so that attribution reaches the creator; we cannot verify that the publisher held the
rights it granted.

**What we excluded, and why.** Material from fal.ai and Replicate (their terms forbid compiling a
collection from their output), every vendor documentation example that carries no reuse grant, and the X/
Twitter clips that aggregators bundle without a licence for the media. That is why this repository holds
50 community cases rather than the several hundred that exist.
