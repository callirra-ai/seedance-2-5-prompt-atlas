## Prompt

```text
Wet black ink gathers on blank paper and rises into a coiled dragon; whiskers uncurl stroke by stroke as the wash bleeds into the fibres. The camera tilts slowly up the body and holds as the last horn forms. A single lamp skims the paper in a dark studio, dust hanging in the air. Sumi-e ink-wash animation, dry-brush texture, generous negative space, no outlines, monochrome with one red seal.
```

## Why it works

Sumi-e lives or dies on restraint, so the prompt spends its words on medium and negative space instead of detail: wet ink, bleeding edges, no outlines. The only motion asked for is whiskers uncurling, which is slow enough for a five-second shot to hold cleanly, and naming one raking lamp keeps the paper texture legible rather than flattening the wash into grey. The wide 21:9 frame gives the body room to coil without crowding the paper.
