Wet black ink gathers on blank paper and rises into a coiled dragon; whiskers uncurl stroke by stroke as the wash bleeds into the fibres. The camera tilts slowly up the body and holds as the last horn forms. A single lamp skims the paper in a dark studio, dust hanging in the air. Sumi-e ink-wash animation, dry-brush texture, generous negative space, no outlines, monochrome with one red seal.
