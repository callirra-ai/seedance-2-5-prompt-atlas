## Prompt

```text
The shield lights first as a thin hex lattice across a raised forearm, then hardens into a curved plate the instant something heavy strikes it. The impact shoves the figure back half a step, boots scraping a concrete corridor, and the lattice ripples outward in rings of light before it fades to nothing. The camera pushes in from the side at hip height and stops dead at the moment of contact. Blue-white shield glow is the only light in a dark corridor. Audio: glassy crack, low thump, ringing fade.
```

## Why it works

An impact needs a before and an after inside five seconds, so the shield is written in three states: thin lattice, hardened plate, fading ripple. The recoil is given to a real body, boots scraping concrete, which anchors an otherwise weightless effect and keeps the exchange free of any injury detail. Pushing in from the side and stopping dead at contact makes the hit the end of the camera move rather than something that happens off-frame.
