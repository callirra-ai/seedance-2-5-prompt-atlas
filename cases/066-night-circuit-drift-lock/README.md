## Prompt

```text
Rear tyres break loose first: a coupe enters the tight left-hander far too fast, the car rotating as the driver holds a long opposite-lock slide and clips the inside kerb. The camera is planted on the tarmac at the kerb and stays still, letting the car sweep past and away down the straight. Night circuit under sodium lights, headlight beams cutting the smoke, tyre smoke glowing orange. Damp-looking asphalt, 35mm grain. Audio: engine at the limiter, tyre squeal, gravel ticking.
```

## Why it works

A drift is a rotation, so the prompt says what turns and in what order: rear tyres, then the car, then the apex. The camera is deliberately the only still thing in the shot, which makes the speed legible and stops a model from panning the whole frame along with the car. Ten seconds fits the entry, the slide and the exit, and pairing sodium light with headlit smoke gives the tyres a background to be read against.
