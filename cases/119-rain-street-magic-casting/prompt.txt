Both hands open over a rain puddle and the water answers: beads lift off the surface, gather into a spinning ring and harden into thin ice that holds for a single breath, then drops back as rain. The camera is handheld and close on the hands, racking focus from the puddle up to the ring and back down. Overcast drizzle, flat grey daylight, wet asphalt throwing pale reflections. Fantasy realism, fine skin and water detail. Audio: faint crackle, rain on stone.
