## Prompt

```text
Pick up from the reference frame, one heartbeat before the freeze. Everything stops mid-motion and holds: a coffee arc hangs solid in the air, a dropped phone floats a centimetre above the pavement, and a cyclist's jacket is caught mid-flap. The camera is the only thing still travelling, drifting sideways through the frozen crossing at walking pace. Mid-morning sun through glass towers, long hard shadows on the pavement. Hyper-real stillness, no motion blur anywhere.
```

## Why it works

Freezing a moment is mostly about what does not move, so the prompt lists three objects stopped in believable attitudes and then says the camera is the only thing travelling. Naming no motion blur is the instruction that makes the frame look like a held photograph instead of a slow-motion shot. Starting from a reference frame means the reader supplies the crowd and the street, and the model only has to stop them.

## Reference image

Upload a busy street photograph shot at eye level with people mid-stride, moving vehicles and clear pavement. Avoid night shots, heavy blur, or a frame where nothing is caught in motion, because the whole effect depends on the still containing movement to stop.
