## Prompt

```text
Two siblings on a sofa, one in their early teens and one a few years younger, both holding controllers; the younger leans forward and jabs a finger at the screen while the older keeps playing, unbothered, then reaches down and pulls the cable, and the room goes quiet. The camera sits low on the floor in front of the television, slightly hand-held. Evening, dark room lit by screen flicker and one lamp. Frayed cushions, tangled cable, half-empty bowl. Game audio cutting out and a controller clattering onto the floor.
```

## Why it works

The argument is staged as escalation through props rather than faces: a pointing finger, then a hand moving to the cable, which is a gesture a video model can render cleanly. Hand-held from floor level gives the shot the loose energy of a home video, and 480p suits that register. The audio instruction is written as a change of state — game audio cutting out — so the sound design carries the punchline the visuals set up.
