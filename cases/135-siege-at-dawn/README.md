## Prompt

```text
Keep the castle and armour exactly as in the reference image. On the wall walk, defenders pass stone-weighted baskets hand to hand while a distant engine arm swings and dust blooms against the outer gate; no one is struck on screen. The camera cranes up from the parapet to reveal the whole curtain wall and the treeline at dawn. Cold blue hour with a low orange sun behind mist. Rough-hewn stone, splintered timber, mail, undyed wool.
```

## Why it works

The shot describes effort rather than combat on purpose: hand-to-hand baskets, a distant engine, dust at the gate, and an explicit instruction that nobody is struck, which keeps a siege scene publishable while still feeling urgent. The crane reveal is the reason this is image-to-video — the reference fixes the architecture, and the model only has to move the camera and animate small human traffic along the wall.

## Reference image

Upload a reference with the architecture or the armour clearly visible — a castle wall, gatehouse or a plain mail-and-wool kit reads best. Avoid reference images containing logos, modern signage or dramatic battle photography, since the model tends to import that intensity and any text it sees.
