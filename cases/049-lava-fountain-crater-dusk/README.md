## Prompt

```text
Molten rock pulses out of the crater in a bright fountain and falls back in heavy glowing arcs, each burst brighter than the last. The camera arcs slowly around the rim at a safe distance, keeping the vent centred and the horizon level. Hot orange light bounces off the underside of the drifting smoke column while the surrounding dark basalt sits in cool blue dusk. Long lens, compressed haze, no people, no aircraft. Audio: deep rumble, crackling hiss, wind.
```

## Why it works

Eruption shots fail when the prompt asks for chaos, so this one gives the fountain a rhythm — pulses, each brighter than the last — and holds the camera on a slow arc at a fixed distance. The two-source lighting note matters too: hot orange from below against cool blue ambient is what makes the smoke read as volume instead of a grey smear.
