## Prompt

```text
Two figures face each other in a rain-soaked courtyard of packed earth and wet stone, one holding a sheathed long sword low, the other still, weight forward on the balls of the feet. The camera holds a wide static frame, then pushes in slowly between them as the rain thickens. Overcast blue-grey light, water sheeting off wooden eaves, puddles pulsing with drops. Lacquered scabbards, hemp sandals, soaked cotton. Steady rain and one slow exhale.
```

## Why it works

The tension is in the stillness, so the prompt asks for two held poses and a single push-in rather than a clash — a model animates restraint far better than choreography. Rain gives the frame constant motion for free, which is why the camera can afford to move so little. The sword stays sheathed: the moment reads as confrontation without any violence on screen, and 480p suits the soft, grainy overcast look.
