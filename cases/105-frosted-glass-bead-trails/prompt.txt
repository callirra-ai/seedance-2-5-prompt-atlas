Tiny beads of condensation swell on the outside of a frosted tumbler, one growing heavy enough to break and drag a clean line down the glass. The camera eases in from a wide view until the frame holds nothing but beads and the wet trails they leave. Cold dawn light arrives at a very low grazing angle, so each bead is rimmed and throws a tiny soft shadow across the etched surface. Vertical framing, shallow focus, dust-free glass, ice visible as blurred white shapes behind. Quiet and clinical.
