## Prompt

```text
A nine-dancer crew in matching bomber jackets snaps into a diagonal formation on the downbeat, arms whipping together, then breaks into a loose travelling step. The camera holds one wide frontal setup for eight counts, then arcs a quarter turn to a second fixed position. Hard overhead strip lights on a black floor, cool white against deep shadow. Crisp music-video grade, sharp highlights, minimal grain. Audio: a mid-tempo pop beat with a clap on two and four.
```

## Why it works

Formation work only reads if the frame stops moving, so the camera is given two fixed positions and nothing else, which leaves the model free to spend its motion on nine bodies instead of a camera path. Naming the downbeat and the arm snap gives the section a beat to land on, and the audio cue points at the same moment. Hard strip lighting separates every limb against the floor, the usual failure point in group choreography.
