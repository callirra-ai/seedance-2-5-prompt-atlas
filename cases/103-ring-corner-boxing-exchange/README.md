## Prompt

```text
Two boxers trade in close: a short left hook lands on a raised guard, gloves and forearms colliding, sweat leaving the brow on the follow-through. The camera orbits them slowly at chest height, keeps both fighters in frame and never cuts. Warm tungsten from a single ring lamp overhead, dark hall behind, smoke hanging in the beam. Square framing, film grain, gritty broadcast texture. Audio: glove impact, hard breathing, shoe squeak on canvas.
```

## Why it works

Contact reads better as geometry than as force, so the prompt describes glove on guard, forearms colliding and sweat leaving on the follow-through, all of which a model can draw without depicting harm. Eight seconds is the shortest length that fits a throw, a block and the reaction after it, and the single slow orbit keeps both fighters readable at once. One overhead tungsten source is named so the ropes stay silhouetted instead of turning into a lit backdrop.
