## Prompt

```text
An attendant procession crosses a red sandstone courtyard toward a carved marble pavilion, carrying folded bolts of silk and a tray of rose petals, footsteps matched. The camera tracks alongside at hip height, then lifts to a wide symmetrical framing of the whole court without cutting. Mid-morning sun, hard shadows on inlaid stone, water channels glittering. Brocade, block-printed cotton, marble inlay, brass trays. Soft drum pulse and many feet on stone.
```

## Why it works

Symmetry is the signature of this architecture, so the prompt spends its camera instruction on arriving at a centred wide rather than on cutting between details. The materials do the period work — brocade, block-printed cotton, marble inlay — while the tray of petals gives the model one soft, slow-moving element to animate against all that stone. Footsteps matched keeps the walking rhythm readable, which is what audio reinforcement supports.
