Eight sprinters leave the blocks as the pistol sounds. The nearest runner's rear foot drives, spikes biting the rubber, arms opening into the first long stride while the rest of the field is still rising. A rail-side camera runs level with the lane at ankle height and holds the gap as they pull away. Overcast morning, flat even light, cool grey track.
