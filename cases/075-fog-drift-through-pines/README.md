## Prompt

```text
Fog banks slide between tall straight pine trunks while the camera creeps forward at chest height along a needle-covered floor, thinning at the front and thickening deeper in. A few needles fall and turn as they drop. Depth is carried by layers of grey, near trunks dark and sharp, far ones fading to nothing. Still, cold morning, no wind, no sun shafts, no animals, no people.
```

## Why it works

This case lives on atmosphere rather than resolution, so a 480p render costs nothing and the prompt spends its words on layering instead: near trunks sharp, far trunks gone. Naming the light as flat and sunless protects that, because a model handed a forest will otherwise invent a warm sunbeam and flatten the very depth it was asked to build.
