## Prompt

```text
Keep the rider, jacket, board and mountain exactly as in the reference image. He carves a hard toe-side turn and the edge throws a wall of cold smoke that hangs and drifts across the slope. A gimbal camera chases low behind the board, then slides out to the side for the exit. Low winter sun straight into the spray, long blue shadows, thin alpine air. Audio: edge cutting crust, powder hiss, wind.
```

## Why it works

The first clause pins rider, jacket, board and mountain to the uploaded frame, which is the part image-to-video loses fastest. One hard turn is enough, a carve, a wall of spray and an exit, so the model spends its capacity on the snow plume rather than on a jump it would have to invent. Shooting into a low sun is what makes powder read as smoke instead of white noise, and the chase camera supplies a scale reference for the plume.

## Reference image

Upload a still of a snowboarder mid-turn, with the full body, jacket and board visible and the slope running away behind them. Avoid flat midday shots with no shadow and avoid a frame cropped at the boots, because the model will keep whatever framing it is given and thin spray reads as nothing.
