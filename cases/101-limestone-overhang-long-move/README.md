## Prompt

```text
High on a limestone overhang, a climber reaches through a long move: left hand slaps a sloper, hips swing out, right heel hooks the lip and he presses up onto the shelf. Shot from above on a fixed line, looking straight down past the rope, the wall filling the frame. Harsh midday sun, hard shadow under the roof, bleached rock, deep blue sky at the edge. Vertical framing, chalk dust hanging in the air.
```

## Why it works

Climbing is a chain of small exact contacts, so the prompt names the hand, the hip and the heel in the order they happen, which is what stops a model from drifting the body into impossible positions. Looking straight down from a fixed line gives the frame a strong graphic shape and sidesteps the hardest thing to generate, feet on a vertical wall. This case is written silent on purpose: it is the one readers drop a music bed under, so the prompt spends no words on sound.
