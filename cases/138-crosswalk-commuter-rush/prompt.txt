Commuters pour across a wide city crossing in both directions, coats and shoulder bags, phones lit, one cyclist weaving at the edge of the crowd. The camera starts high above the junction and descends slowly on a jib toward the middle of the crossing, flattening the walkers into moving shapes. Low morning sun cuts hard across the asphalt and drags long parallel shadows from every person and wheel. Clean 35mm, high shutter, crisp contrast. Audio: signal beeps, shoe scuffs, a bicycle bell, crowd murmur.
