## Prompt

```text
From the reference photo, hold both dancers and the flat horizon; they keep to one slow contact duet on cracked white salt, one lifting the other through a rolling turn, shadows stretching far to the right, then settling back into the opening pose. The camera arcs a half circle at a distance, never closer, no cut. Low golden-hour sun straight down the lens, long hard shadows on white. Desaturated warm grade, gentle bloom.
```

## Why it works

A reference photo is the only sane way to hold two people in contact through a lift, because touching bodies are where generated video tends to merge limbs. The camera stays far and arcs once, which keeps both figures whole and gives the long shadows room to move. Audio is off on purpose: this is a silent-film shot, and asking for music would invite breath and mouth artefacts that nothing else in the frame supports.

## Reference image

A photo of two dancers in a clear held pose on open ground, both bodies fully visible and the horizon line readable. Avoid images where the dancers overlap heavily or are cropped at the feet: contact lifts are where generated video fuses limbs, and a merged reference makes that worse.
