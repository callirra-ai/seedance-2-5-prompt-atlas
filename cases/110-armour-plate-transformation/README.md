## Prompt

```text
Shoulders first: armour plates surface through the fabric of a grey hoodie, the seams splitting along the arms as each segment rotates and locks into the next, and a chair scrapes back as the figure straightens to full height. The camera holds tight on the shoulder, then pulls back fast to take in the finished silhouette against the room. One cold work lamp overhead, hard shadow, everything else black. Audio: cloth tearing, dry mechanical clicks, one long breath.
```

## Why it works

Transformation prompts drift when they describe the outcome instead of the mechanism, so this one is written as a sequence of locks: plates surface, seams split, each segment connects. A specific garment and a scraping chair give the armour something to displace, and that displacement is where the weight comes from. One cold work lamp keeps the silhouette hard and lets the joins hide in shadow, which keeps the result intense without any gore.
