## Prompt

```text
The puppy from the reference photo steps off the last step onto fresh snow and stops dead, one front paw lifted and held, nose dipping to sniff the surface, ears perked forward, then shakes the paw once and looks up, keeping its face and coat exactly as given. The camera crouches at eye height and shuffles backwards as the puppy moves forward. Deep blue dusk light on the snow with a narrow warm strip spilling from the open doorway. Quiet, no wind, no other animals, no people.
```

## Why it works

Puppies multiply legs faster than almost any other subject, so the prompt freezes nearly everything: one paw up and held, nose down, ears forward. That held pose is the peak of the behaviour, and the doorway light gives the model a warm source against blue snow instead of the flat grey that snow usually renders as.

## Reference image

Upload a clear photo of a puppy with the whole body in frame, sitting or standing against a plain background. A shot taken near a doorway or a step works best, because the model needs a floor line for the puppy to step down from.
