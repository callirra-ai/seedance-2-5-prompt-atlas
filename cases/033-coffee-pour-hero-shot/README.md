## Prompt

```text
Keep the product and its label exactly as in the reference image. A thin stream of black coffee pours from above into the cup in slow motion, surface rippling outward, steam rising and catching a warm side light from the right. The camera pushes in gently, no cut, keeping the label centred and legible the whole time. Background stays the same clean surface, slightly darker at the edges. Subtle condensation on the cup, glossy highlight along the stream, commercial colour grade with lifted shadows.
```

## Why it works

Image-to-video cases only hold together when the prompt protects what must not change ('label exactly as in the reference image') and spends its words on the single motion, the light and the camera. One continuous push beats a cut list at five seconds.

## Reference image

Any clean product photo: the cup or package centred, label readable, background uncluttered. Studio shots work best — the model keeps what it is given and only adds the motion.
