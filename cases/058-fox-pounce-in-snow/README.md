## Prompt

```text
Standing motionless in deep snow, a red fox swivels its ears, then leaps nose-first in a high arc and lands face-down, hind legs kicking up behind it, then rises with a mouthful of snow and shakes its head once. The camera drops with the leap, a quick crane move keeping the animal in the lower half of frame. Thin overcast with a pale sun disc, bright grey snow and long blue shadows from the treeline. Natural history look, long lens, clean snow, no other animals.
```

## Why it works

The crouch is half the shot, so the first line is spent on stillness and ear movement before the leap, which gives the jump a launch point. Landing face-down is stated explicitly because a model left alone will tend to land a fox on its feet, and the camera drop mirrors the arc while holding the animal low in the frame.
