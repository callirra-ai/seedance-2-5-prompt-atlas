## Prompt

```text
Continuing the painted frame you uploaded, leave the street and the stall layout untouched. Paper lanterns swing on their wires, steam turns over a noodle pot, a grey cat crosses between the stalls, and two shoppers drift past the left edge. The camera dollies slowly right, no cut, holding the same depth of field. Dusk light with warm lantern pools on wet cobbles. Hand-painted background art, limited cel animation on the moving figures, soft grain.
```

## Why it works

Continuing from a previous frame is the reliable way to keep a hand-painted world consistent, so the prompt protects the layout first and then adds three small independent motions. Nothing is asked to travel far, which is what stops a painted background from warping under motion. One slow dolly keeps the parallax believable at this length, and leaving audio off keeps attention on the texture of the art rather than on footsteps that were never drawn.

## Reference image

A painted background or a previous frame from the same scene, with a clear street line and uncluttered stalls. Avoid frames where a figure already fills the centre: the moving elements need empty space to travel through without covering the art you want to keep.
