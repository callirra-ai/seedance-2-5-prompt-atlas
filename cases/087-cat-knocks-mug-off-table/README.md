## Prompt

```text
Same cat, same markings, same mug on the table; only the paw moves. It stares at the mug, then reaches out one paw and pushes it twice, watching it slide, topple over the edge and drop out of frame while the head tilts down to follow the fall. A fast push-in paired with a slight zoom-out lands as the mug goes over. Warm late-afternoon window light, shallow depth of field, plain wall. Audio: claw on ceramic, a clatter, a small chirp.
```

## Why it works

Comedy timing is the hard part, so the action is split into beats the model can order: stare, tap, slide, topple, drop. Naming one object and one paw keeps the anatomy simple, and the small push-in matched by a slight zoom-out lands on the moment of the fall without the camera racing the subject.

## Reference image

Upload a photo of your cat on a table with a single object beside it — a mug, a glass, a small pot — at least a head-width away. Shoot from roughly table level so the paw, the object and the table edge all share one frame.
