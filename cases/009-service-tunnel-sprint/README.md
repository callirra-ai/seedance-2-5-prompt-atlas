## Prompt

```text
Deep in a service tunnel, a runner in a hi-vis vest sprints along the narrow walkway beside the rails, arms pumping, breath fogging. The camera rides a trolley ahead of them, retreating backwards at knee height so the tunnel walls streak away on both sides. Almost everything is black until a train headlight floods in from behind, rimming the runner and throwing a long shadow down the wall. Gritty 35mm, crushed blacks, handheld micro-shake. Audio: gravel footsteps, a swelling roar, tunnel echo.
```

## Why it works

A chase beat needs legible geography at eight seconds, so the tunnel, the walkway and the retreating trolley are named as one system. The train headlight does the work of forty extras: it hides the set, rims the runner and gives the shadow a direction. Crushed blacks let the model get away with a nearly empty background.
