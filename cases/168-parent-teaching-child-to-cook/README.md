## Why it works

The beat is a small failure followed by shared laughter, and both halves are physical: a hand released, one stir too fast, a shared freeze. That is a performance a model can hold because nothing depends on facial nuance. A nearly static camera at counter height keeps attention on the hands, and image-to-video is used so the two characters and the kitchen stay consistent across the whole take.

## Reference image

Upload a two-character still with an adult and a child together at a counter or table, both at similar distance from camera and lit by window light. Avoid images where the child's face is turned away or hidden, and avoid stock-photo gloss — the model keeps that polish and it flattens the moment.
