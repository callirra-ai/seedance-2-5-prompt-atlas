## Prompt

```text
The launching machine from the reference sheet must stay identical: same plating, same colours, same markings. It hangs in a service gantry while steam vents from the shoulder joints, then drops, lands, and climbs out of the bay on a column of blue flame. The camera dollies along the gantry and then tracks upward with the launch. Cold floodlights from above, orange sparks below. Cel-shaded mecha animation, panel lines, motion blur on the thrusters. Audio: clamps releasing, a deep engine roar.
```

## Why it works

With a reference sheet uploaded the job is to protect the design, so the first sentence locks plating, colours and markings before any motion is described. The launch is split into three physical beats, hang, drop, climb, because a model asked for one continuous transformation tends to melt the joints. Cold floods from above and sparks below keep the silhouette readable against the bay, and the clamp release gives the audio a start point before the engine roar takes over.

## Reference image

A mecha design sheet or a clear three-quarter still of the machine, ideally with the plating markings visible and a plain background. Avoid busy action art or heavy perspective: the model copies what it can read, and a distorted pose will be copied just as faithfully.
