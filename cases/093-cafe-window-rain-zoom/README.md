## Prompt

```text
Rain streams down a cafe window at night while the street outside smears into long red and violet streaks, a cyclist and a bus sliding past as soft shapes. The lens zooms in slowly on the glass until individual drops and the small reflections of the room are the whole frame. Inside stays almost black, lit only by a table lamp and the wet colour coming through the pane. 35mm, heavy bokeh, cool grade. Audio: rain on glass, muffled traffic, a cup set down.
```

## Why it works

A zoom rather than a dolly keeps the street outside in the same geometry while the glass becomes the subject, which is the shot you actually want from a rainy window. Everything beyond the pane is described as colour and shape only, so the model is never asked to draw legible people through wet glass. The black interior gives the drops something to sit against.
