## Prompt

```text
A long serpentine dragon glides between snow-capped peaks below the cloud line, body coiling over a valley, wings barely moving, its shadow sliding across pine slopes. The camera begins tight on the head as it passes a ridge, then pulls back into an extreme wide that shows the full mountain range. Late golden hour, cloud sea under the peaks. Translucent scaled membrane, wet stone, drifting snow. Deep wingbeat and thin high wind.
```

## Why it works

Fantasy creatures look real when the frame gives them something ordinary to pass behind, so the shot starts tight on the head at a ridge and only then reveals scale. Barely moving wings keep it gliding and heavy instead of flapping like a bird, and the shadow on the pines is the detail that proves it is physically there. The 21:9 canvas is picked for the final wide rather than the opening close-up.
