## Prompt

```text
From the pit, a packed crowd throws its hands up as the stage goes black, then a bank of moving lights slams across the front truss and the whole floor jumps on the first beat. The camera pushes forward through the crowd to the rail, one direction, no cut. Beams cut through haze, screens glow in the dark. High-contrast concert grade, crushed blacks, saturated beams. Audio: crowd roar, a kick drum, one held guitar note.
```

## Why it works

Crowd shots work because motion is everywhere and nothing has to be perfect, which is why this one is cheap at 480p and wide at 21:9. The beat is described as a physical event, blackout then lights, so the jump and the beams happen together instead of drifting apart. A single push from the pit to the rail is one direction of travel and easy to hold, and the crowd roar under the kick gives the audio a real room rather than a studio mix.
