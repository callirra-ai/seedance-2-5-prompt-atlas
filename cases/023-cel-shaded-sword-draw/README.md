## Prompt

```text
A masked duelist in a red haori plants both feet on wet stone and draws in one motion; the blade leaves the sheath in a three-frame smear while background lines radiate outward. The camera snaps from a low profile to a fast lateral whip and settles tight on her eyes. Flat overcast dawn light. Hand-drawn cel-shaded 2D animation, hard shadow shapes, bold line work, limited palette. Audio: a short steel ring and one gust of wind.
```

## Why it works

The three-frame smear and the radiating background lines are the parts a video model can actually draw, and together they give the cut a physical beat and a style marker at once. Naming the sheath, the blade and the eyeline keeps a five-second action readable, while flat dawn light stops the model inventing glossy 3D highlights over cel shading. One short audio event means the steel ring lands on the smear instead of drifting across the shot.
