## Prompt

```text
The ring in the reference frame is half-formed, so complete it: keep the sandstone plateau, the two standing figures and the ring's exact position unchanged. The circle closes, turns solid, and pulls a column of sand straight off the ground, grains orbiting its rim while a loose tarpaulin whips flat against the rock. The camera holds a wide locked-off frame and only breathes slightly. Harsh noon desert sun, hard black shadows, heat shimmer. Audio: low resonant hum, sand hissing.
```

## Why it works

Starting from a half-formed ring gives the model a state to finish rather than an effect to invent, which is the most reliable way to use a reference frame. The portal is written as physics acting on real objects, sand lifting off the ground and a tarpaulin snapping flat, because an abstract glowing circle gives the renderer nothing to stay consistent about. A locked-off wide keeps the effect's scale honest against the two figures standing in it.

## Reference image

Upload a wide still of the ring already half-built, with both figures and the plateau in frame and nothing yet glowing. Avoid cropped arcs, night shots, or big foreground objects, because the model will spend the clip rebuilding whatever it cannot see in the reference.
