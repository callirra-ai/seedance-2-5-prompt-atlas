## Prompt

```text
Ground fog parts as a colony ship drops through the cloud deck, hull plates ticking with heat, its own floodlights sweeping the valley floor. Grass and standing water flatten outward under the downwash while a weathervane spins itself loose on a fence post. The camera tilts up slowly from the fence line, holding the ship in the upper third as it settles. Pre-dawn blue, fog lit from inside by the landing lights. Audio: low sub hum, wind, creaking metal.
```

## Why it works

Arrival shots are about the ground reacting first, so the prompt puts the downwash on grass, standing water and a weathervane before it describes the hull. The slow tilt from a fence line plants a human scale in the bottom of the frame, and holding the ship in the upper third leaves room for the fog it is still coming through. Ten seconds is the shortest length in which a descent reads as slow rather than as a cut.
