## Prompt

```text
A vertical split frame shows the same patch of forearm skin twice, dull and dry on the left, smooth and hydrated on the right, with no brand text anywhere. The camera holds one fixed centred frame while a soft frontal panel sweeps smoothly from cool to warm. The only other motion is a faint sheen travelling across the right half and settling. Midday studio neutrality turns into a warm glow; pores, fine lines and moisture film stay visible in both halves. Even, honest, advertiser-safe skin realism.
```

## Why it works

Before-and-after work is where generators cheat hardest, so the prompt forbids the usual tricks: no brand text, no smoothing of the before side, and pores and fine lines explicitly kept visible on both halves. Ten seconds and a vertical frame give the light sweep time to read as a transition rather than a cut. The camera stays locked because the split screen is already doing the movement — adding a push would make two vertical halves that no longer line up.
