## Prompt

```text
A lone knight in weathered plate armour rides through the open gate of a stone castle courtyard at first light, reins loose in one gauntlet, a mud-spattered cloak hanging heavy behind. The camera holds a low wide angle, letting horse and rider cross from shadow into sun, then settles as the horse slows to a walk and stops. Low golden sun rakes across wet cobbles, mist lifting off the well. Wool, oiled leather, cold steel, limestone.
```

## Why it works

Period armour fails when a prompt asks for detail the model cannot resolve, so this one names textures a viewer actually recognises — oiled leather, cold steel, wet cobbles — and keeps the action to a single arrival. Crossing from shadow into sun gives the model a built-in reason to change the image without any cut. Audio is off so the shot stays quiet and processional rather than turning into a noisy charge.
