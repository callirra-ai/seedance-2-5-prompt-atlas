## Prompt

```text
Inside a planted freshwater tank, a small shoal of silver and orange fish holds position against a gentle current, fins flicking, while one of them turns a slow half circle and bubbles rise in a steady chain on the right. The focus pulls from the faint reflections on the front glass to a single fish behind it and stays there. Warm light from above the tank against a dark room, deep green plants, clean water, soft bokeh, no hands, no nets, no equipment in shot.
```

## Why it works

The hard part of any tank shot is the glass, so the prompt makes the reflection part of the focus pull instead of something to erase. The fish are given one calm behaviour — a shoal holding position with a single individual turning — which keeps the frame from turning into random drifting, and warm tank light against a dark room supplies the contrast.
