## Prompt

```text
Rolling into a harbour plaza line, a skater pushes twice, ollies onto a low ledge, holds a fifty-fifty grind, then pops out and rides away. The fisheye camera follows on a board directly behind, staying about a metre off the tail the whole way. Flat overcast midday light, grey concrete, no hard shadow anywhere. Nineties tape grade, soft grain, slight wobble at the frame edges. Audio: urethane rattle, trucks scraping, wind over the microphone.
```

## Why it works

A skate line only reads if the trick order is fixed, so the prompt lists push, ollie, grind, pop out and stops there instead of stacking a montage. The follow-board camera and the one-metre distance are the two specifics that keep a fisheye close enough to feel fast without warping the skater. Flat overcast light is deliberate: hard sun on concrete pushes a model toward a glossy commercial look, not a tape.
