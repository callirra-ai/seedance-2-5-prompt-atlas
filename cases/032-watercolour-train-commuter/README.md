## Prompt

```text
A lone commuter in a yellow raincoat leans her cheek against the window of a rural train, watching rice fields slide past. The camera tracks beside the carriage at window height, then eases into a close-up of her reflection as a tunnel mouth swallows the light. Late afternoon sun, warm gold melting into blue shadow. Loose watercolour animation with visible paper grain and bleeding edges, hand-painted backgrounds. Audio: rail clatter, a soft sigh, a distant crossing bell.
```

## Why it works

Watercolour reads as watercolour mainly through paper grain and bleeding edges, so the rendering line names both and separates the hand-painted background from the animated figure. Tying the tracking move to a real object, the carriage, keeps the camera at a believable speed, and the tunnel gives the shot a natural ending inside five seconds. The crossing bell is written as one distant sound so it cannot fight the rail clatter.
