Resting on a shallow lagoon, a dense raft of small wading birds suddenly bursts upward in one simultaneous wingbeat, splitting into two streams and settling into a tight turning formation. The camera whips up after them, then holds while they bank against the sky. It is the last ten minutes before sunrise: the water is pale grey, the birds dark against a deep blue band. Fast, urgent energy, slight blur on the nearest wings. Audio: a rush of wingbeats, water splashing, thin calls.
