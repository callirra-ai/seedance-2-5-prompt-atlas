## Prompt

```text
In a windowless edit suite a technician leans toward a large monitor, tapping one key and squinting at the picture, a paper cup beside the keyboard. The camera pulls back slowly from their face until the whole dark room and the glowing screen are visible. The only light is the monitor flickering blue-white across their glasses, plus a thin red standby lamp on the rack behind. 35mm, deep contrast, gentle bloom. Audio: fan hum, one key click, a chair squeak.
```

## Why it works

The shot is built backwards from its final frame, so the pull-back is the event and no other action has to render. Screen glow on glasses plus one red standby lamp is enough to light a face and keep the room dark, which is far cheaper for the model than inventing practical lights around a desk. The audio cue stays small so it does not compete with the image.
