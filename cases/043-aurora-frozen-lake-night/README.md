## Prompt

```text
Nothing in the frame moves except the sky. A wide static shot holds a frozen lake in the foreground and a low ridge of black spruce on the horizon; above them, green and violet aurora ribbons unfold slowly, brightening, folding over themselves and fading, while stars stay fixed. Frost crystals on the ice catch a faint blue sheen. Long-exposure look, clean blacks, no grain, no camera shake, no people, no buildings.
```

## Why it works

The prompt deliberately forbids camera movement, because a night frame this dark will smear if the model tries to move it. Giving the aurora a full cycle — unfold, brighten, fold, fade — fills eight seconds with a readable event, while the frost detail and the long-exposure note keep the ice from rendering as flat grey noise.
