## Prompt

```text
A thick patty sears on a flat-top grill, fat bubbling and spitting at the crisp edge, a spatula pressing once. The camera rises from plate level to just above the flame line, ending on the charred crust. Orange burner flare licks from below while a hard hood light cuts the rising steam into visible shafts, late-night kitchen atmosphere. Grill marks, glossy rendered fat, a sesame bun beside it, warm commercial gloss. Audio: steady sizzle rising into a short flame hiss.
```

## Why it works

Food reads as food when the prompt names physics rather than adjectives: fat spitting at the crisp edge, a spatula pressing once, steam cut into shafts by a hard hood light. The camera rises instead of pushing in so the steam stays between the lens and the light, which is the only way the shafts appear at all. The audio cue is written as a bed that rises into one accent, which matches a five-second cut and gives an editor a clear moment to cut on.
