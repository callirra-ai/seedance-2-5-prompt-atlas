## Prompt

```text
Steam puffs from a teahouse pot as a round-faced chibi cook skids into a table, arms windmilling, and saves a wobbling stack of bowls with one outstretched foot. The camera stays flat and side-on, then jerks into a tiny comic zoom on the catch. Soft even daylight through paper screens, one red paper lamp glowing behind the counter. Chunky chibi 2D animation, thick outlines, flat colours, heavy squash and stretch.
```

## Why it works

Chibi motion is squash and stretch, so the prompt asks for flat colours, thick outlines and exaggerated weight instead of detail, which is also why 480p costs nothing here. The gag only needs one readable beat, the foot catch, and a flat side-on view keeps the proportions honest instead of letting the model re-render the character in perspective. The tiny zoom is the punchline, so it is written as one small move rather than a cut, and leaving audio off keeps attention on the timing.
