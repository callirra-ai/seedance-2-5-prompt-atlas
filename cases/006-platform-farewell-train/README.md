## Prompt

```text
Keep the platform, the person and the yellow line exactly as framed in the reference image. A train slides through behind them and the lens racks focus off their face onto the lit windows streaming past, then back again. Their raised hand stays small in frame, coat lifting in the slipstream. Overhead sodium strips throw amber pools down the platform while the train adds one cold white wash. Soft 35mm, slight halation, shallow focus. Audio: a gust, a long braking hiss, a whistle.
```

## Why it works

The reference image is protected in the first clause, because any drift in the platform or the person breaks the illusion of one continuous moment. The only real action is a focus pull, which a model renders convincingly and which reads as emotion without a line of dialogue. Sodium overheads against the cold train wash supply the depth a flat station photo lacks.

## Reference image

Upload a photo of a station platform with one person standing near the platform edge, shot at normal eye level with empty space behind them for a train. Avoid frames where the person is tiny or hidden behind signage, since the model copies the composition and only adds the passing train and the focus pull.
