## Prompt

```text
A singer in a plain black jacket grips the mic stand with both hands and leans into the second verse, eyes half closed, one heel keeping time on the monitor. The camera arcs slowly from her profile to a frontal close-up and stops, holding on her mouth and the mic. A single hard backlight rims her hair; the rest of the stage falls to black. Contrasty stage grade, blue and amber split tone. Audio: a dry vocal line over a sparse kick and bass.
```

## Why it works

Singing shots distort when the model cannot see the mouth clearly, so the move ends frontal and close, with the mic stand named as a fixed anchor for the hands. Limiting the camera to a single arc, profile to front, keeps the face from being re-rendered mid-word. One hard backlight through a black stage separates her from the background, and a dry vocal over a sparse beat leaves room for the lip movement to read.
