## Prompt

```text
A girl in a plain school uniform lifts a small brass bell and light spirals up from her heels; ribbons of colour wrap her arms into gloves while her coat blooms open, and the final pose lands hard. The camera orbits her once at chest height, then cuts to a fixed wide for the finished silhouette. Twilight, low sun flaring behind her shoulders. Cel-shaded 2D animation with sparkle particles, lens flare and a star-field backdrop. Audio: rising chime chord, then one percussive hit.
```

## Why it works

Transformations need a fixed order, so the prompt sequences it: bell, spiral, gloves, coat, final pose, and only then allows a cut. The orbit is deliberately short and locked to one height, because long camera travel through fast particle effects is where these shots fall apart. Twilight with a low flare gives the sparkles something to catch, and the chime resolving into a single percussive hit tells the model exactly when the pose should land.
