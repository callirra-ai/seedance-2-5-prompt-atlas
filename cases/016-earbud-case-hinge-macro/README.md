## Prompt

```text
The lid of a matte charging case lifts on its hinge, two small earbuds nested inside, the case matching the uploaded photo exactly, and a single status light breathes on. A probe lens glides along the seam, then racks focus from the hinge to the light in one clean move. A thin linear highlight sweeps the matte edges against a cool blue-grey void with no ambient fill, so only the geometry reads. Soft-touch plastic, micro-textured metal pins, faint dust specks on the glass. Silent, macro, unbranded.
```

## Why it works

Tech macro lives on surface, so the prompt names the finishes the renderer has to hit: soft-touch plastic, micro-textured metal pins, dust specks on glass. Eight seconds buys the two-stage camera move, a glide along the seam followed by a focus rack onto the status light, which is a single continuous idea rather than two shots glued together. The lighting is deliberately fill-free and cold so the object reads as a silhouette first and a product second, and the status light gives the shot its only warm accent.

## Reference image

A macro photo of the closed case, clean and evenly lit, with the seam and hinge visible. Avoid photos where the lid is already open at an odd angle or where a logo is the only sharp element — the model drives the hinge from the geometry it can see.
