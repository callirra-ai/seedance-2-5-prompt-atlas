## Prompt

```text
Hold the dune shape, colours and horizon from the reference image and change only the surface. A steady breeze lifts a thin ribbon of sand off the crest and carries it left along the ridge, dusting the sunlit face while the shaded side stays still. Soft grain slides down the slope in small avalanches. The camera tracks sideways, matching the wind. Warm low-sun palette, fine sand texture, no people, no footprints.
```

## Why it works

Image-to-video lives or dies on what it is told not to touch, so the first clause pins the dune's shape, colour and horizon before any motion is described. The movement is then kept small and physical — one ribbon of sand, a few short avalanches — because large invented motion is exactly what breaks a locked reference.

## Reference image

Upload a desert dune photo with one clean light-and-shadow edge running diagonally across the frame, and no footprints, tents or vehicles in it. Keep the horizon away from the exact middle so the sand has room to move in either direction.
