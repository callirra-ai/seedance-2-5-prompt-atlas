## Prompt

```text
Preserve the stall, the crates and the vendor exactly as photographed. Hands bag green beans and lift them over the counter while steam rolls off a pot at the back, and a shopper steps in from the left to point at a crate. The camera weaves forward at chest height through the narrow aisle, tarpaulins sliding overhead. Overcast dawn light stays flat and silver so the steam reads white. Warm 16mm, natural colour. Audio: crates scraping, vendor calls, a knife on a board.
```

## Why it works

Chest height is where a person walking a market actually holds a camera, so matching the reference photo to that height means the prompt only has to add hands and steam. Protecting the stall and the vendor stops the model redecorating the stall as it moves. Flat silver overcast light holds across five seconds far better than hard sun, which flickers when the camera travels.

## Reference image

Upload a photo of a produce or food stall taken from the aisle at about chest height, with the vendor and some of the produce sharp. Avoid wide overhead shots of a whole market, because the camera walks forward from the position it is given, so that position is the shot.
