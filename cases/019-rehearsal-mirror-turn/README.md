## Prompt

```text
Alone in a mirrored rehearsal room, an actor in a worn shirt paces and speaks lines at the wall, marking beats with one hand. A handheld camera arcs a half circle from behind their shoulder to the front, so the mirror reflection slides into frame and doubles them. Hard late-afternoon sun comes through half-closed blinds, striping the floor and the mirror in bands of gold and shadow. Warm 35mm, slight handheld roll. Audio: murmured lines, a floorboard creak, a chair scrape.
```

## Why it works

The half circle is chosen because it reveals the mirror without a cut, turning one performer into two figures inside a single move. Naming the blinds matters most: hard stripes on the floor and mirror give the model a strong pattern to check itself against, and patterned light hides small artefacts far better than soft light does.
