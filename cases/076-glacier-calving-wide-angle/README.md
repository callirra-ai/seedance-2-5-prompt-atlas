## Prompt

```text
After a few quiet seconds of stillness, a slab of blue ice shears off the right shoulder of the glacier wall with a long crack, drops into the water, and throws a wave that spreads outward while the new face steams with spray. The camera drifts slowly left from a low boat, swaying gently with the water, keeping the whole ice front in frame. Overcast polar light, desaturated blue and white, clean reflections. Audio: ice cracking, a low boom, water surge.
```

## Why it works

Calving needs the pause before it, so the prompt opens on stillness and lets the fall happen a few seconds in — that wait is what makes the drop land. The camera is tied to a drifting boat rather than a drone, which supplies a small believable sway while the wide crop keeps the entire ice front in frame.
