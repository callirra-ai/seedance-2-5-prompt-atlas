## Prompt

```text
Thick dawn mist sits on a river bridge, and a cyclist in a yellow rain jacket pedals across, front lamp a small halo. The camera tracks alongside at handlebar height on a parallel rail, matching their speed so the railing and lamp posts slide past and the far piers vanish into white. Sun is a soft disc behind the fog, so everything is silver with one warm patch on the jacket. Muted 35mm, low contrast. Audio: tyre hiss on wet boards, water lapping, one bell.
```

## Why it works

Tracking alongside at handlebar height keeps the cyclist a constant size, which is the calmest way to show movement in five seconds and the easiest thing for a model to keep stable. The mist does the rest: it removes the far bank, so the model never has to render a detailed skyline. Fog with one soft sun disc is one of the few lighting setups that survives being animated.
