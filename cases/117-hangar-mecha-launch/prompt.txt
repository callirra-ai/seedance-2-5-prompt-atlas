Inside a grey concrete hangar, a masked pilot in a white flight suit straps into an open cockpit as the shoulder clamps lock down around her. The machine rises on its launch cradle, steam venting from the hip joints, then strides once into the open while the hangar doors part ahead of it. A crane camera climbs with the cockpit and settles behind the shoulder. Cold strip lighting above, blue service lamps along the floor. Audio: hydraulic hiss, servo whine, distant klaxon.
