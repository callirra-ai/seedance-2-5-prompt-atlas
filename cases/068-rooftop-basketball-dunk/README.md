## Prompt

```text
A street player drives the baseline, launches off two feet and throws down a one-hand dunk. The camera is a phone held vertical by a friend, tilting up fast from the sneakers to the rim, then loosening as the chain net snaps. Late golden hour on a city roof, long shadows and warm haze between distant towers. Handheld phone look with a faint lens flare, real asphalt and chain texture. Audio: sneaker squeak, chain rattle, one shout.
```

## Why it works

Vertical framing is the whole point of a phone clip, so the prompt fixes the tilt direction and states that the camera is a friend's hand rather than a rig, which keeps the shake human instead of random. The action is a single beat, drive then dunk, with the net snap as the release, so the model does not invent extra dribbles to fill the time. Golden hour is named with its effects, long shadows and warm haze, so the roof reads as a place rather than a grey void.
