## Prompt

```text
Foil peels back from the chocolate bar in your reference photo, the printed sleeve staying face-up and unchanged, until two squares break away with a clean snap. The camera tilts down from the rumpled foil to the fractured edge, holding as cocoa crumbs scatter. A warm tungsten practical sits low on the right, low-key, falling off into deep brown shadow. Tempered chocolate shows a matte snap face and a faint gloss on the moulded side. Audio: dry foil crinkle, then one crisp snap.
```

## Why it works

Eight seconds is used here because the beat is really two actions in sequence: the peel and then the snap, and squeezing both into five usually rushes the second one into a blur. The camera only tilts, never cuts, because the interesting event is a fracture and a cut would hide it. Low-key tungsten is chosen over bright daylight so the foil keeps a warm specular edge instead of flaring white, and the two-part audio cue is written as crinkle then snap so the sound edit has an obvious sync point.

## Reference image

A photo of the wrapped bar with the printed sleeve facing the camera, plus a clean surface underneath. Avoid photos where the wrapper is already torn open or creased beyond recognition — the model animates the tear you show it, so start from an intact wrapper.
