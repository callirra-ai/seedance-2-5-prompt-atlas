## Prompt

```text
Dust puffs as an adult elephant curls its trunk into a dry patch and sprays it over its own back and ears in three slow sweeps, skin twitching where it lands, and the cloud hangs then drifts sideways. The camera rises from trunk height to full height as the dust spreads. Hazy late-afternoon sun filters through the cloud, turning it ochre and softening the horizon. Wide natural-history framing, fine particles, warm earth tones, no other animals, no people. Audio: a low rumble, dust pattering, dry wind.
```

## Why it works

Counting the sweeps turns a messy behaviour into three readable beats, and each one ends with the trunk returning to the ground, which keeps the anatomy simple. The camera rises with the dust rather than the animal, so the frame fills with the ochre cloud instead of empty sky, and the hazy backlight is what makes the particles visible at all.
