## Prompt

```text
Gliding low over long green swell lines, the drone rises and pulls back until a curved bay of dark sea cliffs fills the frame. One wave breaks white along the base of the headland while the rest of the water stays glassy and green. First light rakes in from the left, throwing long shadows down every gully. Cool teal grade, crisp aerial detail. Audio: surf thudding on rock, steady wind, distant gull calls.
```

## Why it works

Leading with the camera lets the model treat this as one continuous move — glide, rise, retreat — instead of a cut it would have to invent. A single named wave event gives the sea exactly one thing to do, which is what keeps aerial water from turning to mush, and fixing the light as a low left-hand rake stops the model defaulting to flat overhead sun.
