## Prompt

```text
A young lantern maker walks against the crowd down a Tang-era night market street, carrying one lit paper lantern on a bamboo pole while a hundred more hang overhead in red and amber rows. The camera tracks slowly backwards ahead of her, then drifts up above the awnings without a cut. Warm lantern light, blue dusk over the rooftops, faint mist. Silk sleeves, painted rice paper, waxed wood, wet flagstones. Crowd murmur and paper lanterns creaking in the breeze.
```

## Why it works

The crowd is the hard part of a period street, so the prompt gives the model three specific anchors — one carried lantern, red and amber rows overhead, wet flagstones — instead of asking for a whole festival. The camera move is deliberately a single track-and-rise: at five seconds a second cut would leave no time to read the material detail. Audio is on because a silent crowd reads as a diorama rather than a street.
