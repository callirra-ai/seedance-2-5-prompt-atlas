## Prompt

```text
A generic high-top running shoe hangs mid-air and turns slowly while the camera circles a half-turn counter-clockwise at sole height, finishing on the outer panel and stitching. A single strip softbox sits behind the shoe, so a bright rim traces the toe, the collar and the outsole while a dark charcoal gradient falls away beneath it. Breathable knit mesh and matte rubber read clearly, dust motes drifting through the beam. Studio product lighting, controlled and windowless. No text or logos appear at any point.
```

## Why it works

Text-to-video has no photo to protect, so the protection moves into the subject description: a generic, unbranded shoe with no text or logos, which stops the model from inventing lettering it cannot spell. The camera does exactly one half-turn at sole height rather than a full orbit, because five seconds cannot complete a 360 without the geometry visibly changing shape. Naming knit mesh and matte rubber gives the renderer two contrasting surfaces to rim-light, and the dark gradient supplies the separation a white sneaker needs.
