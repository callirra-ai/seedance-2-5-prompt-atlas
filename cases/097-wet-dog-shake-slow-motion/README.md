## Prompt

```text
The dog in the reference image keeps its face, coat and markings; only the shake is new. It plants its paws and twists its body from the shoulders back in one fast shake, loose skin and ears swinging a half beat behind, a halo of water flying off the coat in slow motion and freckling the floor. The camera stays handheld at floor level and lets the shake happen. Cool daylight from a high window, wet floor, clean background, no people. Audio: the shake, droplets on tile.
```

## Why it works

The shake is one of the few animal actions with an unmistakable peak, so the prompt marks it: the twist starts at the shoulders and the skin follows a half beat later, which is the detail that makes it read as real rather than a wobble. Keeping camera movement minimal leaves the model's whole budget for the water.

## Reference image

Upload a clear photo of your own dog, whole body in frame with all four legs and the tail visible, standing on a plain floor. Avoid busy backgrounds and other animals or people — the model keeps the face, coat and markings, so anything else in shot tends to be kept as well.
