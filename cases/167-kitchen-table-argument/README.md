## Prompt

```text
Two people in their forties sit across a kitchen table with breakfast half eaten; the one on the left keeps talking with both hands flat on the wood while the other stops chewing, sets down a fork, and looks at the window instead. The camera stays low and close at table height, drifting a few centimetres left, no cut. Flat grey morning light through net curtains. Chipped enamel mugs, formica, cooling toast. Room tone, a ticking clock, one chair creak.
```

## Why it works

No line of dialogue is written, because a model cannot lip-sync a script but can absolutely read a fork being set down. The beat is a single reaction — one person keeps going, the other disengages — and the mirrored hand positions give the animator two clear states to move between. The 4:3 frame and table-height camera keep both faces close without a cut, and eight seconds leaves room for the pause after the fork lands.
