## Prompt

```text
Two colleagues in charcoal suits walk toward the lens down a long hotel corridor, the taller one talking with quick open hands while the other listens and nods. A gimbal rig retreats ahead of them at chest height, holding both in the lower third as brass wall sconces slide past the frame edges. Warm sconce pools alternate with cold blue exit signs, late evening. Anamorphic 40mm, fine 35mm grain, shallow focus. Audio: soft footsteps on carpet, one low voice, a door latch clicking.
```

## Why it works

A ten second take only holds if the people have continuous business, so the prompt gives them a conversation with hand movement and lets the camera do one predictable thing: lead them backwards. Naming the sconces and exit signs as the light sources stops the model flooding the corridor, and the anamorphic look is described loosely on purpose so the renderer picks its own flare.
