## Prompt

```text
Two hands pull a glossy translucent slab of slime slowly apart, the sheet thinning into a window of bubbles before it tears. The camera follows the stretch upward and stops dead the instant it snaps, letting the recoil finish in frame. A coloured gel light from underneath pushes through the material so the stretched middle glows turquoise while the surface keeps hard wet highlights. Tabletops are matte grey, nothing else in shot, an unhurried evening studio. Audio: a long wet squeak, then one clean snap.
```

## Why it works

The satisfying part of this material is the last two seconds, so the prompt builds towards it: thinning into a bubble window, tearing, then a recoil that the camera refuses to chase. Freezing the move at the snap is what stops the model from drifting across the frame at exactly the moment the eye wants stillness. Underlighting is the reason the stretch reads as translucent depth instead of as a glowing blob, and the wet highlights on the surface give it the tacky skin the material needs to look real.
