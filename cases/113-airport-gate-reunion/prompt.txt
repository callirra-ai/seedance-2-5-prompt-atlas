A man in his sixties stands at an arrivals gate holding a cardboard name card he no longer needs; a young woman with a duffel bag sees him mid-stride, stops short, drops the bag strap and runs the last ten metres. The camera holds at chest height inside the crowd, then tracks with her through blurred shoulders. Late afternoon light through terminal glass, long reflections on polished floor. Creased travel clothes, scuffed wheels, tape on the sign. Gate announcements and rolling suitcase wheels.
