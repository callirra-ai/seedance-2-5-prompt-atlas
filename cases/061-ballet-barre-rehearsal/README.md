## Prompt

```text
In the reference frame keep the barre, the mirror and the leotard colour unchanged. She rises onto pointe at the barre, finds her balance, then unfolds into a slow arabesque while the mirror doubles the shape behind her. The camera stays in a fixed three-quarter wide with a very small push in. Morning light through tall windows, dust in the air. Soft film grade, cool shadows. Audio: a piano phrase, one breath, the creak of the barre.
```

## Why it works

Uploading a studio frame removes the hardest part of a ballet shot, the room, and lets the prompt spend its words on three specific things: the rise onto pointe, the held balance and the unfolding arabesque. The mirror is named because it doubles the shape and quietly helps the model keep the pose symmetric. A nearly static camera protects the line of the body, and the barre creak plus a piano phrase give the audio a small, believable room instead of a concert hall.

## Reference image

A rehearsal-studio frame: dancer at the barre, mirror visible, floor line clear, room tidy. Avoid action shots mid-jump and heavy motion blur, because a blurred reference is a pose the model cannot hold and will keep re-interpreting.
