A courier in a soaked grey jacket sprints down a narrow alley at night, splashing through puddles, one hand pressed to a satchel. Handheld camera runs low and close behind the shoulder, then swings wide as the runner vaults a stack of crates. Audio: hard rain, running footsteps, distant traffic, a single shouted word.
