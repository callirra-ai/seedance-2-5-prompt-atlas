## Prompt

```text
A courier in a soaked grey jacket sprints down a narrow alley at night, splashing through puddles, one hand pressed to a satchel. Handheld camera runs low and close behind the shoulder, then swings wide as the runner vaults a stack of crates. Neon signs in magenta and cyan reflect off the wet asphalt; steam curls from a vent on the left. Shallow depth of field, 35mm film grain, cool highlights against warm sodium streetlight. Audio: hard rain, running footsteps, distant traffic, a single shouted word.
```

## Why it works

One continuous camera move with a physical action the model can anchor to (run, splash, vault) keeps the frame stable; naming the light direction and the lens stops it drifting into a generic night city, and the audio line gives the model something to sync rain and footsteps to.
