## Prompt

```text
Behind a cafe counter, a young barista starts writing on a cup before the customer has finished ordering, then glances up and stops; the older regular taps two fingers on the counter and gives a small nod. The camera holds a tight over-the-counter two-shot at chest height with almost no movement. Warm morning light through a front window, steam off the espresso machine. Ceramic, worn wood, brushed steel. Grinder hum, milk steam, one cup set down.
```

## Why it works

This is a five-second beat about being known, so the tell is the barista writing the order before it is spoken. The camera barely moves by design: the intimacy comes from the tight two-shot and the 3:4 frame, which crops out the rest of the room and puts both faces on one vertical axis. Small, specific sounds — grinder, steam wand, cup on wood — build the cafe without a single word of dialogue.
