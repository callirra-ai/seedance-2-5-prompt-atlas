## Prompt

```text
A candidate in a slightly too-tight jacket answers a question, then realises mid-sentence that it was the wrong one; her hand closes on the folder in her lap, she straightens, and the interviewer's pen stops moving. The camera sits at the far end of the table on a slow push-in that never reaches her face. Cool overhead office light, one window behind, plants out of focus. Laminated desk, thin carpet, loose paper. Air conditioning hum and a pen clicking once.
```

## Why it works

The emotion is embarrassment, which plays as a physical sequence: a hand tightening on a folder, a spine straightening, a pen going still in someone else's hand. Ten seconds is requested because the beat needs a slow build before the freeze. Holding the camera short of her face makes the scene more uncomfortable than a close-up would, and the flat office lighting keeps the focus on the two performances rather than the look.
