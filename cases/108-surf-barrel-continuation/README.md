## Prompt

```text
Continue from the reference frame: hold the surfer's stance, wetsuit, board shape and board colour exactly as shown. The wave keeps breaking over him, the lip throwing forward and closing behind as he trims higher into the barrel. The camera stays almost still at water level inside the tube, drifting only a hand's width with the swell. Backlit morning sun glows green through the curtain. Water beads and spray on the lens. Audio: hollow barrel roar, spray hiss.
```

## Why it works

Image-to-video protects identity first, so the opening clause names exactly what must not change: stance, wetsuit, board shape, board colour. The rest of the prompt spends its words on the water, because the barrel is the effect, and a near-static water-level camera stops the model from cutting away to a beach wide. Backlit morning sun is given as a colour, green through the curtain, which is the one detail that makes a wave look translucent instead of grey.

## Reference image

Upload a still of a surfer already inside the barrel, ideally backlit so the wave curtain is visible in front of them and the board is fully in frame. Avoid wide beach shots, heavy spray across the lens, or a crop that hides the board and the feet, since the model can only continue what it can see.
