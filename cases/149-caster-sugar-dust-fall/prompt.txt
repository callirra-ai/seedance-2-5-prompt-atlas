Fine white sugar leaves the lip of a spoon and falls as a continuous curtain, individual crystals catching and losing the light, mounding softly where they land. The camera sits below the stream, looking up through the falling dust. A narrow slit of light between two black flags cuts the curtain out of a dark backdrop, with only a soft top light for the mound. Airborne particles stay crisp and granular, mid-afternoon. Audio: a dry continuous hiss, then a soft pat as the pile settles.
