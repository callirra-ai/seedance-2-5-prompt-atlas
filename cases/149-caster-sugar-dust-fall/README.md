## Prompt

```text
Fine white sugar leaves the lip of a spoon and falls as a continuous curtain, individual crystals catching and losing the light, mounding softly where they land. The camera sits below the stream, looking up through the falling dust. A narrow slit of light between two black flags cuts the curtain out of a dark backdrop, with only a soft top light for the mound. Airborne particles stay crisp and granular, mid-afternoon. Audio: a dry continuous hiss, then a soft pat as the pile settles.
```

## Why it works

Powder is hard because it either renders as fog or as static noise, so the prompt asks for a continuous curtain with individual crystals catching and losing light, and keeps them granular rather than soft. Shooting from below turns the fall into a tall vertical shape, which is why the frame is 9:16. The narrow slit of light is the real technique here: two black flags isolate the stream so the grains have something to be bright against, and the audio pairs a continuous hiss with one soft landing beat.
