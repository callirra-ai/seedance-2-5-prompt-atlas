## Prompt

```text
Dusk, and a teenager in a grey hoodie swings down an iron fire escape on a brick apartment block, trainers ringing on each rung, one hand dragging the rail. A small camera is mounted on the ironwork above, angled steeply down, so their hands and shoes sweep through the frame on every rung. The sky is blue hour and the apartment windows behind the ironwork are warm rectangles, laundry moving on a line. 35mm, cool shadows. Audio: metal clang, quick breath, a distant dog.
```

## Why it works

Mounting the camera on the ironwork is why this works vertically: the climber passes the lens instead of the lens chasing them, so there is no room for a drift. Blue hour with warm windows behind the ironwork separates the figure from the building without any key light. Clanging metal and breath keep the beat physical.
