A warehouse ceiling becomes the floor. A dropped wrench stops mid-fall, hangs for a beat, then accelerates upward past a workbench; dust and water droplets reverse direction in the same instant, and a mug of coffee shears sideways into a floating ribbon. The camera rolls a full half-turn with the room and ends upside down, still tracking the wrench. Flickering fluorescent tubes overhead, hard top-light from what is now the floor below.
