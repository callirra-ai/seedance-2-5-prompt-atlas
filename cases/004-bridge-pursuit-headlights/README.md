## Prompt

```text
Far out on a long concrete bridge, a dark coupe cuts between lane markers, swerving around a slow truck. A long telephoto on the shore holds the shot and pans right, flattening the span so the car crawls across the frame while its headlights streak. The last blue-grey daylight is going, and the only warmth is tail lamps and oncoming headlights on wet tarmac. Anamorphic 35mm, oval bokeh, fine grain. Audio: engine drone, tyre whine, wind buffeting the microphone.
```

## Why it works

The telephoto is the whole idea: a long lens flattens the bridge so the car seems to crawl even when it is fast, which is exactly what five seconds can sell. The pan is described as slow and continuous to keep the model from inventing cuts. Naming tail lamps and oncoming headlights as the only warm sources keeps the dusk cold and readable.
