## Prompt

```text
A volleyball player in a plain navy kit winds up and drives the ball over the net; the impact frame holds two beats while the ball stretches into a white streak. The camera cuts from a low baseline angle to a fast push on her face mid-shout, then a slow-motion tilt across the court floor. Bright overhead gym panels. Retro 1990s television anime look, visible cel grain, saturated flats, painted gym background. Audio: crowd roar, ball thud, squeaking shoes.
```

## Why it works

A retro television look is a set of specific tells, so the prompt names them directly: cel grain, saturated flats, a painted background, and the old 4:3 frame to match. The held impact frame and the ball stretched into a streak give the cut its physical beat, which is what keeps a five-second sports shot from reading as a generic rally. Cutting from the baseline to a push-in and then relaxing into slow motion mirrors how broadcast replays are actually assembled, so the model only has to hold three simple states.
