## Prompt

```text
Three dancers form a loose circle on a flooded street, trading short footwork bursts while the others keep time with their hands; spray kicks up at every landing. Handheld camera circles the cypher at waist height and dips low as the last dancer drops into a freeze. Cold street-lamp pools, wet asphalt throwing colour back upward, light rain. Rainy-night grade, deep blues, wet skin highlights. Audio: a boom-bap break, splashing steps, one shouted call-out.
```

## Why it works

Handheld energy is asked for but on a fixed radius: the camera circles at waist height instead of cutting, so the model never has to invent new geometry for the street. Spray and splashing are written in because they are the cheapest honest proof that feet are hitting the ground. Naming the break and the call-out turns the audio into an event the edit can follow rather than a bed of noise, and one freeze gives the shot an ending.
